Movie Review

SNOW WHITE I just finished watching Disney’s Snow White (2025), and I have a lot of mixed feelings about it. On one hand, I totally get what they were going for modernizing the story, giving Snow White more agency, and making it relevant to today’s audience. But on the other hand, it didn’t fully land for me. Rachel Zegler was solid in the lead role. She has a beautiful voice and brought a softness and sincerity to Snow White that felt genuine. But at times, the script didn’t give her much to work with. The new songs were okay, catchy in parts but honestly, I missed the charm and simplicity of the original tunes. Gal Gadot as the Evil Queen was stunning, of course, but I didn’t really feel the menace. It felt like style over substance. Visually, the movie was beautiful. The costumes, the forest, the magic mirror all looked great. But the CGI dwarfs? I’m sorry, but they were kind of weird. They felt more like background decorations than real characters, which was a letdown since they were so lovable in the original. Overall, I appreciate Disney’s effort to retell the story with a modern lens, but this version didn’t quite stick the landing for me. It’s watchable, but not unforgettable.
