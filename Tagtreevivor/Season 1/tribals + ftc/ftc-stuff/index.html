Drock's Speech: When the game first started I was already in a major disadvantage as I didn’t know the game started. I was lucky my tribe won the first 2 challenges but it all changes at the 3rd tribal where I knew I was a target. When tribal started I tried to talk to people as the only person I had good connections with was gavino right off the bat. I was a little worried but I decided to talk to Shannon and after convincing her that keeping me will help the tribe for tribe swaps I was able to stay as they soon realized keeping someone that was not in the best shape socially was not best for their game and I was able to stay with a vote of 5-1. I was able to convince people to keep me by myself. When the swap happened I knew that vote paid off as I was lucky to be with the people I trust. Every seemed to like Robert and wanted to keep him but as soon as they got word he was gunning for me they flipped on him and got him out. From there we were able to steam roll the rest of the swap and kept Shannon safe by throwing comps. At merge it was a nightmare on day 1 as I lost immunity and there was a vote to get me or Ghoody out and lucky I was able to stay. After that vote I knew it’s time to see where peoples heads were at as after the first merge vote that’s where the game started as it’ll show where loyalty lies and I knew I was at the bottom. The two people I talked the most were Dino and treb after the vote to see where their heads were at. Treb slowly gain trust to me and he told me everything and we both knew taking out Jim has to be the right callas he was controlling all the votes up to that point. For Dino it was a little tricky as I did vote him and he voted me twice however we both agree they won’t expect us to work together as I did propose a truce and he agree and from there we voted together and we no longer were gunning for each other. This is were I like to say my game did a full 180 as I went to Austin and let him know about an idea I had which was having 2 alliances but be loyal to the OG roborascals and he loved the idea. I told Austin that if we team with Treb and Dino it’ll help us have the numbers, control the votes, and once we hit final six it’ll help us get to the end. Austin loved the idea and was down, but when I told him let’s tell the Rob alliance he didn’t want to which got me worried a little. The alliance wasn’t made just yet as we needed to see if both Treb and Dino would be loyal and to test that we told them the vote was their close ally horse. Both were hesitant as they said that they were close to horse that’s were the double alliance plan helped as I went to the Shan and gav and let them know the vote was horse that why if Treb or Dino goes and asked them we will have the numbers and they will have the confidence to take them out and next thing you know it happened and horse got out but that’s not all I already put in my vote before Treb told me that horse had an extra vote and if you vote him first you get it and I got it. I was able to guide the horse vote and I was able to get the votes to do so. The king vote was another successful vote as we full on gain the trust for all members even tho king did vote me I was able to convince him mer was the vote and he was out with the extra vote. I didn’t mind as it was going to make me look like a threat. The Dino vote was the one I was surprised but reveled as I was actually getting in Trebs head making him think me, Austin, and Dino were a three person in reality we were not and because of that Treb mistakenly took the shoot at Dino. The Austin vote out was a hard one as I did consider him my friend and wanted to go to the end ,but when he didn’t want to reveal the 2 alliance plan and he didn’t tell Shannon she was going to get the votes I knew I had to let him go as I didn’t want people to link me to Austin so I had to make the hard decision of voting him.
